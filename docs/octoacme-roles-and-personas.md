# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide executive oversight, strategic direction, and resource authorization. They champion the project at senior leadership levels and ensure alignment with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and major scope changes
- Allocate budget and authorize resource assignments
- Remove organizational blockers and political barriers
- Review milestone deliverables and project health
- Make final decisions on trade-offs between scope, schedule, and resources

### Reporting Lines
- Reports to: Executive Leadership / C-Suite
- Direct reports: May have authority over Project Managers and functional leaders
- Collaborates with: Product Managers, Project Managers, Business Analysts

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business strategy and deliver expected ROI
- Maximize resource utilization across the portfolio
- Maintain executive stakeholder confidence

### Typical Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Weekly or bi-weekly status briefings, escalation resolution, resource approval
- **With Product Managers**: Quarterly roadmap reviews, strategic alignment discussions
- **With Developers**: Typically indirect through Project Manager, occasional visibility sessions
- **With QA Lead**: Indirect through quality metrics and release readiness reports

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive steering committee updates
- Approval gates at major project milestones
- Strategic decisions via email or brief meetings
- Quarterly portfolio reviews

---

## Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Solution Architects define technical strategy, system design patterns, and integration approaches. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with enterprise architecture standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design high-level system architecture and integration patterns
- Evaluate technology choices and establish technical standards
- Identify technical risks and define mitigation strategies
- Review technical designs for quality, scalability, and security
- Mentor developers on architectural best practices

### Reporting Lines
- Reports to: Engineering Leadership / CTO organization
- Collaborates with: Developers, QA Lead, Project Managers, Security teams
- May lead: Technical design review boards

### Goals
- Deliver robust, scalable technical solutions
- Minimize technical debt and maintain architectural consistency
- Enable team velocity through clear technical direction

### Typical Interactions
- **With Developers**: Daily design reviews, pair design sessions, architecture decision records
- **With Project Managers**: Weekly risk reviews, technical milestone planning
- **With QA Lead**: Test strategy alignment, integration testing approach
- **With Business Analyst**: Requirements feasibility assessment, technical constraint identification

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture decision records
- Design review sessions and whiteboarding
- Technical spike presentations
- Architecture review board participation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality strategy, test approaches, and acceptance standards. They coordinate testing activities and ensure deliverables meet quality bars before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards for projects
- Coordinate test planning and execution across features
- Review acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Manage test environments and automation frameworks
- Report quality metrics and release readiness
- Lead bug triage and severity assessment

### Reporting Lines
- Reports to: Engineering Leadership / Quality Assurance Director
- May manage: QA Engineers, Test Automation Engineers
- Collaborates with: Developers, Project Managers, Solution Architects

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Minimize production defects through comprehensive testing
- Improve test coverage and automation over time

### Typical Interactions
- **With Developers**: Daily PR reviews, test case reviews, bug reproduction
- **With Project Managers**: Weekly quality reports, release readiness assessments, risk identification
- **With Solution Architect**: Test strategy alignment, non-functional requirements validation
- **With Business Analyst**: Acceptance criteria refinement, test scenario development

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup participation and bug triage
- Test plan reviews and coverage reports
- Release go/no-go recommendations
- Quality metrics dashboards

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers facilitate organizational adoption of new processes, systems, or ways of working. They assess change impact, develop adoption strategies, and measure success.

### Responsibilities
- Assess change impact across affected teams and stakeholders
- Develop change management and communication plans
- Coordinate training and onboarding activities
- Monitor adoption metrics and address resistance
- Partner with project teams to ensure smooth rollout
- Document lessons learned and refine change processes

### Reporting Lines
- Reports to: Operations Leadership / Chief Operating Officer
- Collaborates with: Project Managers, Business Analysts, Training teams
- May coordinate: Cross-functional change networks

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and minimize disruption
- Ensure stakeholders are prepared and supported through transitions
- Reduce resistance and accelerate time-to-value

### Typical Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Weekly coordination on rollout plans, stakeholder identification
- **With Business Analyst**: Impact assessment, user persona analysis, training needs
- **With Developers**: Understanding feature changes, demo sessions for training material
- **With QA Lead**: Validation of user acceptance testing and training environments

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder impact assessments and readiness reports
- Change communication campaigns (emails, presentations, town halls)
- Training schedules and materials
- Adoption metrics and feedback summaries

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They elicit requirements, document processes, and ensure solutions deliver intended business value.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Analyze current state processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Define acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Create process flows, user stories, and requirement specifications
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops and requirement reviews
- Validate solutions against business needs

### Reporting Lines
- Reports to: Product Management or Business Operations
- Collaborates with: Product Managers, Project Managers, Developers, QA Lead
- May support: Multiple project teams simultaneously

### Goals
- Ensure complete, accurate requirements that reflect business needs
- Bridge communication gaps between business and technical teams
- Maximize business value delivered by technical solutions

### Typical Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Daily collaboration on backlog refinement, success metrics definition
- **With Project Managers**: Weekly status updates, dependency identification, scope clarification
- **With Developers**: Frequent requirement clarification, user story refinement, demo reviews
- **With QA Lead**: Acceptance criteria validation, test scenario development
- **With Change Manager**: Business impact analysis, training content development

### Typical Communication
- Requirement specifications and user stories
- Process flow diagrams and use cases
- Stakeholder workshop facilitation
- Demo sessions and acceptance reviews
