# OctoAcme Role Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist helps new team members quickly understand their role, responsibilities, and how to work effectively within OctoAcme's project management framework. Use this to ensure consistent onboarding and reduce ramp-up time.

## General Onboarding (All Roles)

### Week 1: Orientation & Access
- [ ] Complete company-wide onboarding and HR setup
- [ ] Receive access to necessary tools (GitHub, project boards, Slack/Teams, documentation)
- [ ] Review [OctoAcme Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md)
- [ ] Review [OctoAcme Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — read your role and related roles
- [ ] Meet with manager to discuss role expectations and initial priorities
- [ ] Identify your project assignment(s) and primary team members
- [ ] Join relevant communication channels and recurring meetings

### Week 2: Project Context
- [ ] Review current project charter(s) and one-pager(s)
- [ ] Understand current sprint/iteration goals and backlogs
- [ ] Review the project's RACI matrix (if available) or [RACI template](./octoacme-raci-template.md)
- [ ] Attend standup, planning, or team sync meetings
- [ ] Shadow a more experienced team member in your role
- [ ] Review relevant technical documentation and architecture diagrams

### Week 3-4: Active Participation
- [ ] Begin taking on tasks appropriate to your role
- [ ] Contribute to at least one team meeting or review session
- [ ] Complete your first deliverable or milestone
- [ ] Request feedback from peers and manager
- [ ] Identify any gaps in documentation or process (share with Project Manager)

## Role-Specific Onboarding

### Project Sponsor

**Focus**: Strategic oversight, governance, decision authority

- [ ] Review portfolio of projects under your sponsorship
- [ ] Meet with each Project Manager to understand project health, risks, and resource needs
- [ ] Understand budget allocation and approval processes
- [ ] Review escalation paths and decision-making authority
- [ ] Establish cadence for status updates and steering committee meetings
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders and communication expectations
- [ ] Review business case and ROI expectations for each project

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Project charters and business cases
- Risk registers
- Monthly status reports
- Budget and resource allocation spreadsheets

---

### Solution Architect

**Focus**: Technical design, architecture standards, risk identification

- [ ] Review enterprise architecture principles and technology standards
- [ ] Understand current technical landscape and system integrations
- [ ] Review recent architecture decision records (ADRs)
- [ ] Meet with engineering leadership to understand architectural priorities
- [ ] Attend technical design reviews for active projects
- [ ] Identify current technical debt and improvement opportunities
- [ ] Set up pair design sessions with developers
- [ ] Review security and scalability requirements

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- System architecture diagrams
- Technical design documents
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technology stack and standards documentation
- Security and compliance requirements

---

### QA Lead

**Focus**: Quality strategy, test coverage, release readiness

- [ ] Review test strategy and quality standards
- [ ] Understand Definition of Done for current projects
- [ ] Access test environments and automation frameworks
- [ ] Review test coverage metrics and quality dashboards
- [ ] Meet with developers to understand testing workflows
- [ ] Attend sprint planning to understand upcoming features
- [ ] Review bug tracking process and severity definitions
- [ ] Understand release process and sign-off requirements
- [ ] Set up test automation tools and CI/CD integration

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Definition of Done
- Bug triage and severity guidelines
- CI/CD pipeline configuration
- Quality metrics dashboards

---

### Change Manager

**Focus**: Adoption strategy, stakeholder readiness, communication

- [ ] Understand organizational change history and culture
- [ ] Review current and upcoming change initiatives
- [ ] Identify stakeholder groups and their change concerns
- [ ] Review change communication templates and channels
- [ ] Meet with Project Managers to understand rollout plans
- [ ] Assess training needs and existing training materials
- [ ] Understand adoption metrics and success criteria
- [ ] Review past retrospectives for lessons learned on change management
- [ ] Establish communication cadence with project teams

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Change impact assessments
- Communication plans
- Training materials and schedules
- Adoption metrics and dashboards
- Previous change retrospectives

---

### Business Analyst

**Focus**: Requirements gathering, stakeholder facilitation, value delivery

- [ ] Review backlog and understand prioritization approach
- [ ] Meet with Product Manager to align on product vision
- [ ] Understand stakeholder landscape and their needs
- [ ] Review requirement templates and user story format
- [ ] Attend requirement gathering sessions or stakeholder interviews
- [ ] Review acceptance criteria and validation process
- [ ] Understand current state processes and pain points
- [ ] Set up sessions with developers to understand technical constraints
- [ ] Review business metrics and success criteria

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Product roadmap and backlog
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Process flow diagrams
- Business requirements documents
- Success metrics and KPIs

---

### Project Manager

**Focus**: Coordination, scheduling, risk management, stakeholder communication

- [ ] Review project charter and scope
- [ ] Understand project timeline, milestones, and dependencies
- [ ] Meet with Product Manager to align on priorities
- [ ] Review risk register and mitigation plans
- [ ] Understand team composition and resource allocation
- [ ] Attend or schedule kickoff meeting if project is new
- [ ] Set up recurring meetings (standups, syncs, retrospectives)
- [ ] Review project board and workflow states
- [ ] Establish status reporting cadence with stakeholders
- [ ] Review [Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md), [Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md), and [Execution](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) guides

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Project charter and one-pager
- Project plan and Gantt chart
- Risk register
- RACI matrix
- Communication plan

---

### Product Manager

**Focus**: Product vision, prioritization, success metrics

- [ ] Review product vision and strategy documents
- [ ] Understand customer personas and user needs
- [ ] Review product roadmap and backlog
- [ ] Meet with stakeholders to understand business priorities
- [ ] Review success metrics and current performance
- [ ] Attend user research sessions or review insights
- [ ] Understand competitive landscape and market trends
- [ ] Set up 1:1s with Project Manager and engineering leads
- [ ] Review past retrospectives and lessons learned

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- Product strategy and vision documents
- Roadmap and backlog
- User research and feedback
- Success metrics dashboards
- Competitive analysis

---

### Developer

**Focus**: Feature implementation, code quality, collaboration

- [ ] Set up local development environment
- [ ] Review codebase structure and conventions
- [ ] Understand branching strategy and PR workflow
- [ ] Review CI/CD pipeline and automated tests
- [ ] Attend code review sessions
- [ ] Pair with experienced developer on first task
- [ ] Understand Definition of Done and quality standards
- [ ] Review recent technical designs and spikes
- [ ] Complete first small PR and incorporate feedback

**Key Artifacts to Review**:
- README and setup documentation
- Code style guides
- Architecture diagrams
- PR templates and conventions
- Test coverage reports

---

## 30-60-90 Day Goals by Role

### First 30 Days: Learn
- Absorb context, shadow others, ask questions
- Understand processes, artifacts, and team dynamics
- Complete onboarding checklist items
- Deliver first small contribution

### Days 31-60: Contribute
- Take on full responsibilities with guidance
- Identify and address gaps in documentation or process
- Build relationships with cross-functional partners
- Deliver meaningful work independently

### Days 61-90: Lead
- Operate autonomously in your role
- Mentor newer team members
- Propose improvements to processes or artifacts
- Demonstrate full role competency
